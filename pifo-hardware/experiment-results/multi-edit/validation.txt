Claims NOT supported by these measurements:
- Work/web have no backlog: 13 packets per tenant are measured at t1 in the
  p1-starting runs.
- Untouched-path delays are indistinguishable from either control: Gmail, Ssh,
  Http and Https all exceed the paired p95 absolute-delay threshold of 10
  cycles versus both steady p1 and steady p2 in the Rio run.
- New tenants receive their first service within one or two request cycles.
  Even the already-configured p2 control first serves them 38--53 cycles after
  t1; Rio measures 96--210 cycles.
- The saturated 6/17 realtime share is measurable after its queue drains:
  only 0.20 is offered, so the report measures settling to that offered load.

The 512-cycle teardown and 513-cycle install reset budgets are model costs,
reported separately from hardware instruction counts. The source-side queue
is unbounded but measured. Frozen-subtree copy is supported; full live-survivor
ascent and brain-state migration are not claimed.
